Simple Garlic Oil Pasta
Serves 2

Ingredients:
- 8 oz spaghetti
- 3 Tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- pinch red pepper flakes (optional)
- salt for pasta water
- handful chopped parsley
- grated Parmesan to taste

Boil pasta in well-salted water until al dente. Drain, reserving 1/2 cup pasta water.
Warm oil with garlic and chili until fragrant (do not brown the garlic).
Toss pasta with garlic oil, a splash of pasta water, parsley, and cheese. Serve immediately.
